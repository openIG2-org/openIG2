# 2026-10-16: the campaign's InitStory and inline stories, replayed against ig2.exe

Spec: `re/rules/campaign.md` §8. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/campaign.rs` and the story-fleet helpers in
`story.rs`. Replay: `tests/campaign_trace.rs`. Tracer: `tools/trace/run_story.py --campaign N` + `story.js`.

## What was recorded
The tracer spawns the GOG `ig2.exe`, sets the race menu's state (`FajValasztasStartGame` 0xBBB17C, the opening
animation at 100 % 0xBBB16C, race 0xBBB178, difficulty selector 0xBBB180, the debug seed 0xB7725C) and lets the
main screen's own update (`MainScr_Kilep` → `CampaignGameStart` 0x4f5e90 → `InitNewGame` → `InitStory`) start the
campaign. The scripts the port doesn't have (`st_Begin/Rnd/BRnd/Fable/Line/Elem/Multi_include`) are patched to
`ret`, so the exe runs the engine, `st_Scen_include` and the inline stories of `Story_Check`, like the port.
`MEGNYERES`/`MEGHALAS` count and return; `st_ShowAnim` and `SaveRegistrySetting` only log.

Records: **init** (`InitStory`, with a snapshot after `LoadScenario`), **tick** (`FRISSIT_Story`), **check** (a
hook's outermost `Story_Check`) and **staged** calls. Staged calls run natively: `IG2_gameUpdate`'s call of the
frame hook (exe 0x4e0523) is pointed at a stub that, after the frame, runs a job the agent wrote (the calls, with
callbacks into the agent for the snapshots), so the Interceptor hooks see the game's code inside them (they don't
fire inside the agent's own `NativeFunction` calls). The fuzzer starts the inline stories (402..918) with their
inputs set up (`StoryAdat+0xcc`, `AktFleet`, `AktPlanet`, variable 4059), sends specials 0x11/0x12, story 404's
arrival at a fleet, story 405's message answer, raw daily checks and `WIN`s; between records it runs story
counters out (4001..4003, 4011 with random steps, 4035, 4038, 4039, 4051, 4061), toggles which empires the human
knows, gives the human's designs refit targets (404) and moves human fleets next to story 403's base.

Every record lists the calls the stories made to the rest of the game (`acts`, with depth and return value:
`FlottaNew`, `FlottaMozgat`, `FlottaDelete`, `HajoNew`, `TankNew`, `st_IsInRR`, `st_GetTerv`, `AddWormhole`,
`st_BolygoDestroy`, the owner change, `st_AddMoney`, `TervAutoDesign`/`TervUjraSzamol`, the volcano, the diplomacy
special, the tutorial destroyer, `FlottakUjraSzamol`, `NewMessage`, `SaveRegistrySetting`,
`EpuletNewSearchLocation`, `LoadScenario`, `Generate_StarNames`) and what `st_fAddShip` read (the fleet's owner,
the design's ground flag); the config and registry values `InitStory` read (overridable: `--registry`,
`--storyline`, `--storymap`).

## Result
All **34,959 records exact**: the whole story state (including the new message payload, `DisableAddShip`,
`st_fNewEx`'s `last_fn_*`, the storyline story and map), stream 5, `MEGNYERES`/`MEGHALAS`, and every call the
exe's stories made, in order and with their arguments (floats bit-exact). Coverage: 8 campaign starts (all three
campaigns, registry fail/1/2 and the config override; both mirror flags, campaign 2's extra buildings), 21,737
ticks, 10,494 hook checks, 2,720 staged calls (750 story starts, 659 specials, 632 flag checks, 326 arrivals,
353 `WIN`s); 320 counters ran out, 2,491 stream-5 draws, 39 `MEGNYERES`, 27 `MEGHALAS`. Story 400 started its
heroes (one twice, see below), storylines 530/550/560/680/720 and 21 random stories; 401 went through all its
steps (episodes 1, 4, 6, 8, 10, 20, 22, 24 and its end), with `st_AddKolonizalo`'s fleets; the exe made 231
`FlottaNew` (story 403's escorts, 404's trader, 905's fleet, 918's and the colonisers'), 221 `st_IsInRR`, 1,872
`st_GetTerv`, 2,589 `HajoNew`, 75 `TankNew`, 500 fleet orders (403's patrol, chase and recall, 404's refit trips
and stops, 905), 66 wormholes, 238 refit rounds, 85 owner changes (903), 26 volcanoes, 30 tutorial destroyers, 13
diplomacy specials, 18 money drains, 32 messages, 14 × 720 design resets (908), 1 planet destroyed (402) and 18
storyline registry writes. Not checked against a call: the 4 undo actions of finished stories that reach other
systems (here `Diplomacy_RemoveSpecial` of story 910; the undo list itself is compared).

Findings on the way (spec'd, kept):
- The race menu's own update starts the campaign once its flags are set; the tracer's redirected-return
  trampoline never ran (the frame hook's return slot isn't the one `this.context.esp` points at), hence the
  call-site stub.
- A hero can start twice: story 400 picks among the heroes that aren't *running*, and a hero picked earlier in
  the same loop is only queued (`StartVodor`), so it is picked again and lands in its bucket twice (story 181 in
  the first campaign trace).
- `LoadScenario` gives the story engine the planets' `pAzon` ids as well as `vSet` lines.
- `Generate_StarNames` (stream 1) gives planets the story ids 500.. and sets variable 500.
- Campaign 3's `StoryElejeSeq` row overlaps campaign 1's `StoryLine` row (harmless: campaign 3 has no 401).
- Story 403's counter 4035 is tested inside the daily block, so it fires only when a counter and the daily
  flag meet in one check (never seen); escort id 4034 falls outside the exe's array.

**43 of 61 caught.** The 18 survivors:
- Unreachable in a campaign: story 400 skipping running heroes (no hero runs at the driver's start);
  `st_fNewEx` mode 1 with a planet in `param >> 16` (the inline stories pass 0); the coloniser search's mode 1
  (it only differs from mode 0 for `id == 0`); the rebuilt escort's `st_fAddTank` (the fleet ids 4030..4033 have
  `flotta.txt` records, so `AutoAddship` sets `DisableAddShip` and the explicit ships and tanks are skipped).
- Equivalent with this data: the `last_addship` bookkeeping (`AutoAddship` fills every story fleet the inline
  stories make and the end of `Story_Check` clears it), `AutoAddship`'s story filter and "mark only when
  filled", the story level thresholds (counts equal at neighbouring levels), `StoryError` after
  `st_BolygoDestroy` (overwritten by later accessors).
- Boundary-only: episode 9's scan (the human always knew an empire when 8 or 9 came up), 403's chase weight and
  range (9 chases), 404's budget per day and nearest-ship tie, `st_fNewEx`'s speed at exactly 1.0, the wormhole
  angle constant (π vs 3.141592 changes the truncated offset rarely; the asm pins `0x852368` = π).
