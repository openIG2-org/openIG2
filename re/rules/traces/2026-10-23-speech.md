# 2026-10-23: the speakers, the message speech and the alien voices (ig2.exe)

Spec: `re/screens/audio.md` §5. Probe `tools/trace/probes/speech.js` with `run_screen.py --kep 2 --probe … --sounds …`
on `quick-1.sav` (human empire 6, music volume 100); output `_local/traces-audio3/speech{3,4,5}-stdout.txt`.

## Method
The probe replaces `Fake_FRISSITES` (exe 0x4de720) and, after the original, calls the exe's own functions on the
starmap. Calls made from a Frida listener callback (and, as it turned out, from a replacement too) don't reach
sound.js's `PlaySound`/`Speech::Play` hooks, so the probe reads the state the calls leave: a `Speeches` set's cooldown
(`SpeakingDirector` → `Speaker` {+0 sets, +4 id, +8 next} → `Speeches` {+0 count, +4 list, +8 cooldown, +0xc kind,
+0x10 next}), the `MessagesHang` globals, the `Mes_Hangok` records' handles (`+0x100`). The probe's counter `n` runs
per `Fake_FRISSITES` call (about 1.5 to 2.4 a UI tick); `ui` is the UI counter 0xB772A4.
