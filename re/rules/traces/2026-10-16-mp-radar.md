# Radar in multiplayer mode, and the f64 booster sum (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/fleet-movement.md` §6 (radar and visibility). Port: `ig2-sim::radar` (`RadarTables::refresh`,
`Starmap::refresh`) with `StarmapContext::multiplayer`. Test: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/fleet_trace.rs` (now also
`fleet-mpradar.jsonl`), `radar::tests::booster_sum_in_f64_equals_f32_on_reachable_inputs`. Tracer:
`tools/trace/run_fleet.py --fuzz-radar --mp-radar` + `fleet.js` `mpRadar()`.

## MP radar staged in SP

`GetENRadarKifejlesztve` runs at the start of `FRISSIT_Starmap` (exe 0x594afc), so the whole radar pass sees the
`MultiPlayerMode` byte (0xBAE2C0) of that call. `--mp-radar` sets it to 1 for half of the refresh ticks
(`Ido2 % 10 == 1`), only for the duration of the `FRISSIT_Starmap` call (restored in `onLeave`), after making one or two
random planets unowned radar stations (owner 0, level 2..7); `--fuzz-radar` perturbs the rest as before (alliances,
owners, stations, stealth, boosters, story fields). The snapshot records the flag, the replay passes it to the port.
The MP branches: no story radar bonus for the human, an unowned radar planet observes as empire 0 (SP: as `EN`, in
both the fleet and the planet visibility passes), no SP extra circle of the human's for level ≥ 2 stations.

| Recording | Ticks | Radar refreshes | In MP mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fleet-mpradar.jsonl` | 377 (a fuzzed attack opens a battle screen, which stops strategic time) | 38 | 13 |

Result: every tick matches (133 fleet radars > 0, 215 fleet and 298 planet visibility changes, 17 empires met).
Mutation: the port run in SP mode on the same trace (`multiplayer: false`) fails at the first MP refresh (tick 60:
unowned stations make planets visible to the human). The test's minimum length is now 300 ticks for this reason.

## Booster sum

§6.3 step 4 is `D = min(D, (f32)((f64)(dy² × 2.0 + dx²) + (f64)RadarHatotav2[owner][1] × 1.5))` in the exe
(`cvtss2sd`/`addsd`/`cvtsd2ss` @ 0x594fc2..0x594fca). It can't differ from the f32 sum on any reachable input:
`RadarHatotav2[e][1]` is always a `RadarHatotav` entry (integers in the shipped exe), and an integer below 2²³ times 1.5
is exact in f32; two f32 values whose exponents differ by less than 29 bits add exactly in f64, so the single
`cvtsd2ss` rounds like `addss`. A gap of 29 bits would need `dy² × 2 + dx² ≥ 2²⁹ × lead`, i.e. coordinates beyond
~10⁵, far outside the starmap. So no trace can pin it; the port keeps the exe's f64, and
`booster_sum_in_f64_equals_f32_on_reachable_inputs` checks the equivalence on 200,000 random integral ranges
0..1000 and distances up to ±4000.
