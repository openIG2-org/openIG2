# 2026-10-16: the multiplayer game modes (`st_Multi_include`), replayed against ig2.exe

Spec: `re/rules/campaign.md` §8.10. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/multi.rs` (`MultiStories`). Replay:
`tests/campaign_trace.rs` (the scripts are `ScenarioStories` then `MultiStories`). Tracer:
`tools/trace/run_story.py --campaign N --keep multi --fuzz` + `story.js` (`multiFuzz`).

## What was recorded
The campaign tracer (`2026-10-16-campaign.md`) with `st_Multi_include` left in the exe (`--keep multi`; the other
unported scripts patched out). The campaign never starts the modes, so the fuzzer does: it starts 1001..1006
(1003 and 1005 more often; with `v(10010)`/`v(10020)` 0..3 days and `v(10059)`/`v(10060)` sometimes set) and
`WIN`s running ones so their starts run again; it runs the modes' counters out (10011, 10021, 10031, 10033,
10034), ends ground battles (flag 4, `GrWarBolygo`) on their planets or elsewhere, stages colonisations (special
10, now and then on a capture-the-flag base, which the exe really hands back to owner 0 through `FUN_0059af50` /
`BolygoElfoglal`), answers and promotes the radar offer (message 0x2347, flags 6/7), sends the purchase (special
15, the human's or another player's), runs the radar counter with the human owning the planets and enemy fleets
moved around them, sends fleets of empire 1 or 2 to the bases (`Story_FleetArrivedSave` + `…Check`) and ends space
battles with two or three fake participants and `CTF_value` set. World changes made for one record (planet and
fleet owners, fleet positions, `CTF_value`) are put back after its `post` snapshot.

Extra world fields while the modes are kept: stream 2's seed, `CTF_value` (0xF261EC), every fleet's `+0x52`,
every planet's name, the ships; the setup lists the info string table's ids (0xBAA1F0). Extra calls logged:
`AddSzoveg` (the text), `SetParamStr` (decoded as `PlayerNev[e]`), `Multi_Send`, `HajoDelete`, `TervNew` (with the
design record seen at `TervUjraSzamol`), `st_bUnhide`, `FelfedezBirodalom`, `BolygoInfoAdd`.

## Result
**Exact**: all 9,312 records (the modes running in 9,209 of them) match the exe's story state (buckets, queues,
variables, counters, planet ids, `StoryError`, the undo list, …), streams 5 and 2, `MEGNYERES`/`MEGHALAS` and
every call the modes made, in order and with their arguments; fleets' always-visible bit and the bases' names are
checked against `post`, the flag carrier's record against the exe's (minus its 9 uninitialised bytes). The old
campaign traces (`campaign-c*`) still replay exactly with `MultiStories` in the script list.

Covered: 61 / 58 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 17 starts of 1001..1006 (and 60 / 57 / 7 / 10 / 13 / 15 ends), 54 hill count
lines, 106 wins by a counter (4 of them the human's), 2 three-planet searches on stream 2 (51 tries each; the later
starts found the planets), 26 radar purchases sent, 124 money charges, 263 threat lines (RED/YELLOW/GREEN), 543
fleet visibility changes, 67 flags taken (`HajoNew` of a carrier), 20 touchdowns (`HajoDelete`), 207 score
changes, 67 flags lost or returned by battles, 22 base colonisations undone, 4 flag-carrier designs, 176
`st_bShow`s with their info lines, every text.

Seen here too (and in the storyline traces): `NewMessage` (0x508fe0) calls `SetIdoLelassit(2)`, so a message sent
by a counter slows the game at once and the later counters of the same `FRISSIT_Story` tick at ×1 (the replay's world
takes the exe's resulting speed; noted on `StoryEvent::NewMessage`). A first recording also showed that 1005
reads `SpwFlottak[1]` past a one-participant list (stale fake entries); the game always ends battles with two or
more, the fuzzer now does too.

Mutations (16): caught 11 (the 51 tries, the default hill time, `CTF_value`'s index, flag returns without the
carrier test, the radar range, the score line's read order, the touchdown formula, the third participant, the
per-owner counts of 1002, the take conditions); missed 5, all boundary or data-free: the distance's sqrt in f32
(asm-pinned f64), ties in `st_bGetNearest`, planet slot 0 in `st_GetBirodalomSulypont` (owned by nobody here),
a threat exactly at 80, owner-0 enemy fleets, a view of exactly 0 in `st_bShow`.

Untraced: `st_bShow` meeting an unknown owner (the modes' planets were free), MP itself (`Multi_Send` →
`MULTIGET_Story` → special 15 was staged directly), a 1003 win by the start's fallback planets (1, 1, 1).
