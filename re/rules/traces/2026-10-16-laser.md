# The laser and the story mad tank in the real-time ground war: runtime verification (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/ground-combat.md` §11.10 (the laser, the freed auto-aim target) and §11.8 step 6 (the story
mad tank). Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/ground/battle.rs` (`shot`, weapon 7), `ground/tick.rs`
(`mad_tank`), `ground.rs` (`set_target`: the building fire range `+0x14c`). Replay:
`tests/ground_rt_trace.rs` (the battle-tick, `FRISSIT_Grwar`, AI and dropship replays now also read
`grt-laser*`; the battle-tick replay checks stream 5 too). Tracer: `tools/trace/run_ground_rt.py --mode
battle --laser [--mad --human --place-after T]` + `ground_rt.js` (Frida 17, GOG `ig2.exe`, `quick.scn`).

## Method
- `--laser`: the staged attacking designs (and two in three defending ones) get the laser pod 0xa5 in their
  first slot (weapon 0x800, `WF_NonSimulateWeapons[7]`: range 14, reload 30); the designs are restored after
  the battle.
- Every call of the beam-end function 0x47af70 on the battle's field is logged as a `beam` event (not
  replayed): target, start, end, the returned point, the hit flag 0xDE01DF and (from `grt-laser-mad-5`) the
  probe: the 3D scene pointer 0x93DA34, the scene-object lookup 0x451210 and the number of `vonalmetszes`
  (0x53a2b0) calls inside.
- `--mad` (with `--human`): before each `WF::MI` the human's placed vehicles get flag 4 (as an `aifuzz`
  record, so the replay carries the write); in one call in six, one idle mad tank's cell is moved within 4
  cells of a random building, so the building pick meets buildings within 7 cells (the reservoir draw).
- The battles are shown on their colony screen (`AktKep 3`, `AktPlanet == WF+0x18c`), so the exe ran its
  mesh test for every beam.

## Result
- **3,123 `WF::Refresh` ticks, 3,124 `FRISSIT_Grwar` passes, 2,925 `WF::MI` calls and 12,342 dropship passes
  and landing orders exact**, with **210 laser shots** fired by the port in the replayed ticks (every unit
  field, the lists, the buildings, streams 2 and 5, the libc sequence). Tolerated: 13 render-only gun
  elevations (`+0x1c8`) off by up to 150 ulps (the tolerance was 128, now 256; same open issue as U23).
- The laser's libc cost: 60 draws per shot for the `villamgenesis` beam object (12 points, 10 inner ones
  jittered twice by 0x616620 = 3 `rand()` each), on screen or not. The earlier port lacked them, so the
  first laser shot of any battle desynchronised the libc sequence.
- The mesh intersection: in `grt-laser-mad-5` every probed beam found the target's scene object and ran
  `vonalmetszes` once; 20 of 28 hit the target's mesh (units and buildings). Each hit lay 97.3–99.2 % of
  the way from the beam's start to the target point, at most 7.0·10⁻⁷ rad off the start→target direction in
  the map plane (f32 rounding), and in front of the shooter. `SetRealDamage` only uses that direction's angle
  against the body direction (sectors at π/4 and 3π/4), so the port's target point gives the same armour
  side; every one of these shots replayed exactly. `grt-laser-3`'s 58 beams never hit (no probe was
  recorded there). The projectile calls (list A within 60, list B within 40, in `WF::Refresh`) only place the
  explosion flash.
- **Freed auto-aim target (original quirk)**: in `grt-laser-2` tick 509, vehicle 5's idle fire
  picked unit 24 and killed it with one laser shot: `RemoveTarget` ran inside the shot, then idle fire stored
  `+0x27c := 24`. `RemoveTankGroup` had `operator delete`d unit 24 but kept its pointer, and from tick 510
  the exe kept reading the freed record (still in range, other owner) and shot at it again at tick 516 (60
  libc draws). The tracer stops recording a unit's region when it dies; the replay now keeps a dead unit's
  last record (`freed-u<i>`), which reproduced the exe exactly. The port keeps dead units' records anyway.
- **Story mad tank**: 1,184 ticks drew on stream 5 for mad tanks, all exact: 131 nearest-enemy rolls (19 found
  a target), 46 building picks (12 with buildings within 7 cells: reservoir draws with sums 1–3), 300 target
  drops. `SetTarget`'s building fire range (`+0x14c := width/2 + range`, `ground-ai.md` §6.9) is now set by
  the port's `set_target`; the existing grt traces still replay exactly with it (29,461 ticks).

## Not covered
- A beam whose angle falls within ~10⁻⁶ rad of a sector boundary, or a shooter inside its target's mesh (the
  only cases where the mesh hit could change the armour side; render-dependent, see §11.10).
- Mad tanks in the campaign itself (flag 4 is staged here; the campaign's story code sets it).
- A freed record overwritten by the heap before a shooter reads it (unreproducible; not seen).
