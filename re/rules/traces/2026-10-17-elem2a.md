## What the replay found
- 802's planet pick and 824's average read `HanyEpulet` (exe 0xBA9518, the buildings), not the slot count
  `HanyEpuletLefoglalva` (0xBA9840) the other blocks loop over: 824 picked another planet when they differ.
- `st_KemEselyLin`'s cases are in the exe (the ELF's jump table hides them): its kind 4 is `40·s₃ + 60·s₂`, not
  `st_KemEsely`'s `70·s₂ + 30·s₃`; the chance is `(f64)(x·m)/100 + a`.
- `st_bGet3Nearest` keeps its three planets in a static that is never cleared: a slot not refilled keeps an earlier
  call's planet.
- The exe inlines `st_SetBirodalomVedelem`, `st_SetSemlegesViszony` and `st_SetHaborusViszony` in 824 and 852 (the
  logged calls differ, the writes are the same) and drops the ELF's assert in 802's planet search.
- The scripts' spy portraits and names read `Kemek[EN][KemkedesAktKem]`, not the spy owner's.
