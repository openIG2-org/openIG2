## Setup
GOG `ig2.exe` (sha256 2baa530b…0442) under Frida 17, `scenario/quick.scn`, speed 3. quick.scn holds
no warships (only 8 unarmed traders, fleets 11..18), so for `auto` and `indit` the agent first builds

`HajoNew` / `TankNew` (0x53d400 / 0x53e800 / 0x541750), from that empire's armed designs.

## Findings from the traces
- `Story_SpaceBattleStarted` (called inside `SpaceWarIndit` before the decision) **resets** the story
  switches (`Story_SpWarTiltas`, `Story_SpWarSimulateTiltas`, `StoryAdat+0x88/…`) before the campaign
  script sets them per battle: two routing mismatches came from fuzzing them beforehand (spec §4 step 2).
- The refresh tick's in-battle reset runs over fleets 1..=n only: slot 0 keeps its bit (spec §6).
- quick.scn is useless for combat as shipped (no warships): fleets have to be built first.

## Mutation check
22 single-expression changes to the port, replayed against all traces: **19 caught**. Not caught,
so pinned by the asm only: shield shares in f32 instead of f64 (`TervUjraSzamol` 0x542656…), a
saturating instead of wrapping u8 capacity, and the defender's `sqrt` kept in f64 in the strength
ratio (0x5a795d `cvtsd2ss`). Caught: engine speed / race-2 bonus precision, the race-5 double
0.7000001, price accumulation, unit hp × 30, the discarded first roll, focus fire, damage carry-over,
fighter-vs-gun immunity, ground vehicles not fighting, the unreduced overflow cut, lowest-index
`TankDelete`, the retreat's `+0.001f` and f32 steps, the retreat itself, the trader exception, the
5:1 threshold, the owner and `!= 0` tests of the marking loop.

## Tooling notes
- Frida 17 did not fire the agent's own Interceptor hooks (rndlog, `SimulateSpBattle`,
  `SpaceWar_NewBattle`) inside calls it made through `NativeFunction`, even with `traps: 'all'`;
  the replays compare full state and the routing probe infers the path from effects (stream 7 rolled;
  screen 0xB, a filled battle slot, or a cleared dummy order).
- The auto probe restores ships, vehicles and the three fleet records after each battle so the few
  fleets survive. Buildings aren't restored (`EpuletDelete` also updates counters, the colony map and
  the planet's ground-war turrets `WF[p]`, ELF 0x11f3630 + (p−1)·0xA20).
- Fuzzed auto-resolves crashed the exe after 13–64 battles in every session (access violations in
  `SimulateSpBattle`; first case: warships pushed into a trader fleet, later cases: planets with many
  fuzz-converted gun buildings, which have no ground-war turrets). These states don't occur in play;
  the fuzz therefore runs in short sessions with several seeds and leaves trader fleets alone.
- Not traced: MP routing, > 2 participants (story `st_AddFleetToSpWar`), `GroundWarIndit` after a won
  planet battle, the human report text.
