# Traders' income in the save replay (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/fleet-movement.md` §4.3 "Trader income". Port: `fleet::Arrivals::trader_paid`, booked by
`colony_side::ColonyArrivals` (money pool 0 with the AI income multiplier, `TradeIncomeThisYear`), inside
`Starmap::tick` where the exe's `FRISSIT_Starmap` (exe 0x594ae0) does it. Test: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/save_replay.rs`.
Tracer: `tools/trace/run_replay.py --traders` + `replay.js`.

## Evidence (exe asm, 0x596f09..0x596fdc)

`test [edi+0x18], 1` skips a trader that has traded; `0x5411a0(f, 1e6, 0, 0)` = nearest planet; `0x541150(n, 0x30, 1)`
/ `(n, 0x31, 1)` = working trade centres / banks; `cmovg` caps banks at 1; `cvtdq2pd` + `mulsd` by 0.5 (0x848530),
`addsd` 1.0 (0x8522e0), `mulsd` by `centres × 10000` and by the race factor (`PlayerFaj[o] == 3` → 2), `cvttsd2si`;
`push 0x2512` / `push 0` / `push o` → `call 0x53fb10` (`MoneyHozzaadLog` → `MoneyHozzaadOld`, pool 0) with the
amount as f64; then `add [o·4 + 0xBAE150], amount` (`TradeIncomeThisYear`, unscaled). The ELF's
`FRISSIT_Starmap` reads the same (`MoneyHozzaadLog(owner, 0, (double)amount, "starmap.cpp", 0x2512)`).
The only other money access in `FRISSIT_Starmap` is the production block after the fleet loop
(`MoneyMennyi(e, 1)`, `MoneyHozzaadLog(e, 1, −x)` at line 0x25ca), so the payment must be booked before it.

## Recording

The existing recordings pay 0 (quick.scn has no trade centres): one payment of 0 in `replay-quick1-trade-x16`.
`--traders` turns the last finished non-hub building of every planet of empires 1..=8 into a working trade
centre (48) and the one before into a bank (49), and moves every trader flying to a planet 0.5 units short of it
with a speed override (+0x3c) of 2, before save 0. Without the override the traders took ~9 ticks to reach their
stopping point and the economy step on tick 5 had already switched the new buildings off (paid 0).

five recordings still pass.

not scaled (`MoneyHozzaadOld` without the multiplier).
