## What was recorded
The campaign tracer (`2026-10-16-campaign.md`) with `st_Elem_include` left in the exe (`--keep elem`; the other
unported scripts patched out as before). The script's 29 blocks not ported yet are patched out too: the `push id`
before each block's `st_Story(id, 1)` call gets an id nothing starts (0x7fff0000), so 820's `StartStory(821)` starts
a story that does nothing on both sides. The fuzzer starts the 18 ported episodes (the multi-step ones more often),
runs their counters out at the next tick or stages them with the world they test set up for the one record (planet
owners, the human's planet count, techs at status 4/5 or with time left, empires 1 and 5 dead, the relation with
empire 5, the human's money, a ship of a design with `+0x34` set, most of the visible owned planets the human's for

their messages, ends ground battles on their planets (flag 4, attacker won or not), reports radar contacts with
820's and 830's planets (flag 11), lands fleets with the ids 8185/8642 (arrivals at a point), ends space battles with
816's raider and the human among the participants, sends specials 10 (colonisation) and 13 (a tech researched),
signals 816's end (818), runs daily checks (817's razing at difficulty 4 with empire-11 planets) and ends episodes
(`WIN`) so they start again. Values written for a record are put back after its `post` snapshot. Before 816 or
818 starts, the tracer runs `StoryInit8160` (exe 0x5c4d40) natively as the storylines do (planet 8160 to empire 10):
without it 816's `AutoAddtank` builds empire 10's tanks on someone else's planet and the game crashes. Story 405 is
started directly now and then (its start silenced by `v(4059)`) for 865/866's test. The episodes' raiders and guards
(owners 10, 11) are kept still so they never start a real-time battle.

Extra world fields while the script is kept: planets' `+0xbb`, `+0x186`, `+0x187` (after st_BRnd's three), the
building slots `[kind, +8]` of the human's and empire 11's planets (`HanyEpulet` 0xBA9840, `Epuletek` 0xBA9E90), the
human's designs' `+0x34`, techs 6 and 161..171 (`+0x34`, `+0x38`, the human's `+0x58 + 4·EN`), the trade goods'
prices (`TradeTervek` `+0x24`), `Viszony[EN][5]`/`[5][EN]` levels and `[5][EN].+4`; plus st_BRnd's (fleets' `+0x40`

## Result
**Exact**: all 58,220 records match the exe's story state (buckets, queues, variables, counters, planet ids,
`StoryError`, the undo list, `StoryAdat`), stream 5, `MEGNYERES`/`MEGHALAS` and every call the episodes made, in order
and with their arguments (new fleets and their ships and tanks, fleet orders, `st_IsInRR`, planets destroyed and
colonised or conquered, buildings placed and deleted, messages and their parameters, info lines, money, the peace of
864's alliance); planets' `+0x28`, coordinates, `+0xbb`, `+0x186/+0x187`, the techs' times, the trade prices, the
relation fields and the global morale are checked against `post`. Starts: 835 574, 842 493, 816 323, 864 313, 866
290, 831 272, 830 265, 820 182, 867 178, 813 169, 837 141, 817 130, 865 129, 818 104, 841 57, 811 52, 868 40; 1,457
counters ran out, 2,750 stream-5 draws; 837 won 20 times, 817 razed 139 buildings, 830 destroyed its planet 175 times,
864 made 313 alliances. The older campaign traces (and begin's, rnd's) still replay exactly.

Found on the way: `WIN`'s undo of type 8 (`st_bSetExtra(−a, 0)`) registers the entry again, and the undo loop
re-reads the count (an entry appended past the cursor is applied once more and lands in the slot just zeroed): the
port's `Story::win` now does the same (§2.3). `st_BolygoDestroy` sets `StoryError` (as 402's and 2311's already did).
Two tracer bugs fixed: several staged jobs armed together (after a stall) shared the fake space-battle participant
list (now written when the job runs), and the building dump stopped at slot 199.
