# 2026-10-16: the trade market replayed against the exe

Spec: `re/rules/trade.md`. Code: `ig2-sim::trade` (the market), `ig2-sim::game` (`Game::trade` in the tick,
`Game::buy`, the `Market` view of the save state), `ig2-sim::save` (the `Trade*` chunks). Test:
`engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/save_replay.rs`. Tracer: `tools/trace/run_replay.py` + `replay.js` (`--stage`,
`--randlog`).

## Method

The whole-tick save replay (`2026-10-16-save-replay.md`) now compares the market's chunks (`TradeHanyTradeCuccVan`,
`TradeTervek`, `TradeHanySzallitas`, `TradeSzallitas[1..18]`, `TradeOldNap`) and the unsaved `OldEtaHonap`/`AktEtaDays`
every tick; `replay.js` also dumps `StoryDesignData` (with the first save), `StoryDesignDataMut`,
`StoryDesignElsoTerv` and `TradeCheat`. The plain recordings never buy anything, so `--stage` exercises the rest
from the loaded save, before save 0:

- a working trade port (kind 50) on the human's last planet (re-targeting goes there), `StoryAdat+0xc4` set low
  (the story merchant comes within a few ticks), five extra offers, every offer dated a year back (past the
  180-day limit, so the daily expiry draws run);
- pending deliveries: own ship designs due in 1, 0 and −1 days, one to an AI planet (re-targeted), a foreign
  design (a new design record), a vehicle design, a fighter design, and one for AI empire 3 to a human planet
  (refunded: no trade port);
- this state is saved as `pre.sav`; then the human buys four times through the exe's own `MULTIGET_Trade`
  (0x6070b0): its two `Multi_Get` calls are pointed at a stub that hands it one staged `TradeSzallitasTemp`
  (`Multi_Send` only queues for a later frame), price from the exe's `TradeAr`. One accepted purchase with the
  screen's delivery time, one buying the offer's remaining units (sold out), one of the sold-out offer and one to
  an AI planet (both rejected).

The port loads `pre.sav`, replays the four messages with `Game::buy` (same accept/reject), must equal save 0, and
then replays every tick.

## Result

All 1,640 ticks match byte for byte (every modelled chunk and the compared unsaved globals), the purchases
included. Over them: 15 arrivals (existing and new design records, ships into new fleets, vehicles into fleets
and garrisons, a fighter unit, `Story_Special(0x13)` each), 2 refunds (an AI's, scaled by its income multiplier),
2 re-targetings, 4 accepted and 4 rejected purchases, 6 offers (random and story, hull-tech and generated names),
13 expiries and the monthly libc draws.

The libc draw exposed three hand-offs the tick driver lacked (none is trade's own, all now in `game.rs`):

1. **The economy step's prologue draws libc.** `FRISSIT_Planet(true, 0)` (exe 0x4749b9..0x474ab0) sets each
   planet's ground-battlefield weather `WF[p]+0x8d0` (one `rand()` for surfaces 2, 5, 7) and ambience `WF[p]+0x8d4`
   (exe 0x47a380, one `rand()` when the colony map is below `hanyterep`, 0x9B2EC8 = the `terepleiro.txt` count),
   skipping the planet on screen (`AktPlanet` 0xB9933C when `StarmapMode` 0xB9998C == 1). `--randlog` counted 135
   draws before `FRISSIT_Trade` on an economy day (41 + 94, one planet on screen), which put the exe's
   `AktEtaDays` at draw 135. Ported as `Game::battlefield_weather` (draws only; the battlefields aren't ported).
2. **Building blocks grow by 20.** `EpuletNew` (exe 0x53c7d0) reallocates a full block to `block + 22` records and
   sets `HanyEpuletBlokk += 20`; the save carries all `block + 1` records, the new ones uninitialised heap. A slot
   appended into a block keeps bit 7 of the record's old flags. `SaveState::store_colony` grows blocks this way
   and lists the new records; `Game::fill_fresh_buildings` gives them `Game::uninit` bytes at the end of the
   tick (records past the used slots entirely, used ones in the bytes no ported code writes: `+2, +3, +0xb,
   +0x14..+0x17`, and flag bit 7).
3. **Fleet arrivals reach the story.** `Story_FleetArrivedSave(f)` runs on arrival and again after arriving at a
   followed fleet (and merging into it), and `Story_FleetArrivedCheck()` at the end of that fleet's step
   (`FRISSIT_Starmap`, exe 0x596492 / 0x596ed7 / 0x59706c); the check is skipped when the arrival started a
   battle. `StarmapEvent::Arrived` / `ArrivalResaved` now carry what the save stores; `Game::story_arrivals`
   runs them (after the starmap loop: nothing a scenario story does there is read by later fleets in it).

Production's unit creation also got the original's uninitialised bytes where the save replay first met a
human-side delivery: `FlottaNew` clears fleet `+0x2c/+0x2d`, `HajoNew` clears ship `+4`, appended fleet and ship
records come from `Game::uninit` (`Fresh::Fleet` / `Fresh::Ship`).

## What comes from the exe

- `DesignPoenosanElnevez`'s localised text for a story offer's generated name (`TradeEvent::OfferNamed`): the test
  copies the name bytes from the exe's save (the port's own text is the hull tech's name, the function's last
  fallback).
- `StoryDesignData` (from `designs.txt` at start-up) comes from the live dump.

## Mutation check

Caught: no expiry (tick 2), half refund (tick 2), no economy-prologue libc draws (tick 23). The three hand-offs
above and the building block growth were found as failures before they were added (story arrivals, building
blocks grown by one, appended building flags without bit 7).
Not distinguishable: the price factor as `r as f32 / 100.0` instead of `(f32)((f64)r / 100.0)` (equal for every
`r` in 70..=140).

## Not covered

Deliveries arriving at a planet captured the same tick (the arrival uses the planet owner's fleets, as
`Legyart` does), `TradeCheat`, a full market (40 offers) or a full delivery list (50), the story discount
(`StoryAdat+0x60`), `OsszHanyTerv[e] < 0x29` for the random merchant's empire, MP. The traders' income
is booked since (`2026-10-16-traders.md`; 0 in these runs).
