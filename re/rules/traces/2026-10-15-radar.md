# Radar and visibility: runtime verification (2026-10-15)

Spec: `../fleet-movement.md` §6 (and §3). Target: GOG `ig2.exe` (sha256 2baa530b…0442),
`FRISSIT_Starmap` @ 0x594ae0 (refresh block on `Ido2 % 10 == 1`) and `GetENRadarKifejlesztve` @ 0x53e020.

## Method
`tools/trace/fleet.js` now also records, before and after every hooked `FRISSIT_Starmap` call:
`RadarHatotav` + `RadarHatotav2Init` (live), `RadarHatotav2`/`…Negyzet`/`RadarHatotavSzorzo`/`…Negyzet`,
`BirodalomIsmeri`, `OsszHanyKolonizalo`/`…Terraformalo`, `ProgramPlayers`/`ProgramPlayers2`, the per-empire
state of radar techs 0x21..=0x24, the tutorial globals, and per planet the radar level (`+0x6e`), story
notice (`+0xbb`), visibility (`+0xd8`, i16 × 19) and finished colony hubs. Designs (already dumped whole)
give radar level `+0x6a` and stealth `+0x6b`.

`run_fleet.py --fuzz-radar` (with `--fuzz`, fixed-seed LCG, every 40 ticks): random alliances (sometimes
one-sided) and break-ups, planet owners, radar stations (levels 2..=7) and their removal, planet
visibility cleared or set to −1, planet story notices, forgotten `BirodalomIsmeri` entries, radar techs
granted/revoked, design radar levels and stealth, fleet notice/radar-ignored/hidden bits, radar-booster
story ids (1011/1021/1311), and the story radar fields (`StoryAdat+0x28/+0x38/+0x48/+0x50`).

`engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/fleet_trace.rs` starts every tick from `before` (radar tables, met matrix,
pools included), runs `Starmap::tick` (radar tables every tick; §3 + §6 on refresh ticks; movement) and
compares bit-for-bit: every fleet field incl. visibility, radar range and flags; planet radar levels,
visibility and notices; the four radar tables; `BirodalomIsmeri`; the pools. Only the space-war
in-battle bit is an input (the fleets that have it in `after`). The live `RadarHatotav`/`Init` are also
checked against what `ig2-rules` reads from the exe file (equal).

## Findings
1. `ProgramPlayers2` is 18 after loading quick.scn (16 in the exe image): read it live.
2. Production (§7, after the movement loop) counts a newly built colony ship into
   `OsszHanyKolonizalo` straight away; the replay credits fleets built that tick.
3. A colonisation arrival (`BolygoElfoglal`) sets the new owner's planet visibility to **1000** and
   takes the colony ship out of the pool (pool −1): colony-system effects, left to that port.
4. The first tick after a load has all-zero multiplier squares and row 0 (the tables are only filled
   by the first `GetENRadarKifejlesztve`), matching `InitNewGame` (rows 1..=18, multiplier only);
   `RadarTables::new_game` does the same.

## Enemy approaching: same-tick slow-down (added later on 2026-10-15)
Question (STATUS U18): `SetIdoLelassit` (0x541270) writes `IdoSpeedSzorzo` at once. Asm: the radar block's
"enemy approaching" call (`push 1; call 0x541270` @ 0x59556e) precedes the movement loop, and the step
reloads the global for every fleet (`movd 0xbae25c` → `cvtdq2ps` → `mulss` @ 0x596424), so the whole
tick's movement runs at the slowed speed. The port kept the old `speed_mult`.

`run_fleet.py --fuzz-approach` (×16; the frame hook sets ×16 again after each slow-down): on 2 of 3 refresh
ticks, 1–2 fleets become unseen attackers of the human (owner a live AI empire or the trader itself,
attack order on a human radar planet or on a fleet turned human, placed at 0.3–0.9 of the human's radar
radius of that planet, `vis[EN]` cleared, story id ≠ 0x1F88); on 1 in 5 of those ticks the SP speed lock
`nemlehetidotallitani` (0xBAD0D4) is set for the call. The next tick cancels the staged orders: an
attack reaching the human opens the real-time battle/ground-war screen and strategic time stops (the
first attempt stalled after 54 ticks that way). The trace also records the lock and `ProgramSebesseg`;
the replay now checks `IdoSpeedSzorzo` after every tick.

| Trace | Ticks | Refreshes | Slow-downs (16 → 1) | Locked refresh ticks | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fleet-approach.jsonl` | 1,996 | 200 | 57 replayed (+1 on a skipped battle tick) | 26 (approaches kept at ×16) | exact |

Mutations: no slow-down (the old port) → 279 mismatches; speed lock ignored → 39. Fix: `StarmapContext`
gains `speed_locked`/`program_speed`, `Starmap::tick` takes `&mut` and calls `slow_down()` after the refresh
when an `EnemyApproaching` was raised (nothing else in the refresh reads the speed);
`time::slowed_down` is shared with `ResearchContext::slow_down`.
