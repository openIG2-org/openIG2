# Fleet merges and ship-carrying arrivals in the save replay (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/fleet-movement.md` §4.3, §4.6. Port: `fleet::Arrivals::{merge_units, delete_units}` called inside
`Starmap::tick` from `Starmap::merge` (`FlottaOsszevon`) and `Starmap::delete` (`FlottaDelete`); `game::StarmapHooks`
carries them out on the combat view (`spacewar::CombatWorld::{merge_units, move_ship, new_vehicles, delete_ship}`),
counts `OsszHanySajatFlotta` down (the `[0xFF]` = `Tankok[96].count` re-entry bug included), moves `AktFleet`, and
the arrivals' `Story_Special` calls run in order before the fleet's arrival check (`StarmapEvent::StorySpecial`).
Test: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/save_replay.rs`. Tracer: `tools/trace/run_replay.py --merges` + `replay.js`
`stageMerges()`.

(ELF decompile) calls `Story_Special(10 / 0x18 / 0x10 / 0x14 / 0x12, …)` before each `FlottaDelete`.

## Findings

- `HajoAtpakol` was not ported in the starmap: the merged fleets kept their old totals. Ported; the merge's
  bit-5 rule now sees the merged ship count, and the `OsszHanySajatFlotta` decrement and the `AktFleet` move
  (saved) were missing too.
- `strcpy` of the name copies up to the NUL only (the port copied all 21 bytes).
- `FlottaDelete` from the starmap (colonisation, terraforming, scripted arrivals, dead empires, empty fleets)
  left the ships and vehicles in place and the per-owner fleet counter unchanged.
- The arrivals' `Story_Special` calls (10, 0x18, 0x10, 0x14, 0x12) were not run.
- **Every `LoadSave` ends with `FlottakUjraSzamol()`, after writing a save too** (ELF `save.cpp`): the tracer's
  per-tick saves recount totals and pools in the exe. With a colonising fleet the pools differ (the order took
  the colony ship out of the pool, the recount puts it back): the replay now calls `Game::saved()` after each
  traced save. (Every save the player or the autosave makes does the same.)
- `Game::with_ai` stored the starmap's stale pools over the AI's (the AI keeps them in the combat view).
- `FelfedezBirodalom` stores `PlayerKi[e]` at contact-slot `+0x04` (`PlayerSlot::controller`), and
  `FRISSIT_Diplomacy` sets `MostHanyPlayerVan = MaxPlayerek` every tick (`Diplomacy::shown_players`).
- The colonising order's `FlottaMozgat` freed colony ships / terraformers (`StarmapEvent::ColonyShipFreed`) never
  reached the pools in `Game::starmap`; they do now.
