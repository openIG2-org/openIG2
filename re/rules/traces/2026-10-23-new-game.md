# 2026-10-23: the campaign start replayed against ig2.exe

Spec: `re/rules/new-game.md`. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/game/new_game.rs`. Test: `tests/new_game.rs`.
Tracer: `tools/trace/run_newgame.py` + `newgame.js`.

## What was recorded
The tracer spawns the GOG `ig2.exe`, waits 1200 frames on the main menu, sets the race menu's globals (as
`story.js --campaign` does: `FajValasztasStartGame` 0xBBB17C, `FajVSzetnyilas` 0xBBB16C = 100.0, race 0xBBB178,
difficulty selector 0xBBB180, debug seed 0xB7725C) and lets `MainScr_Kilep` call `CampaignGameStart` 0x4f5e90.
Around that call it takes the whole `.data` section (0x8de000.., 0x7924e4 B) before and after, then calls the
exe's `LoadSave(97, "NewGame", 2)` (0x543c80). Logged: `InitNewGame`'s arguments, the `srand` seeds during it
(`RandomInit`'s and `NEWGAME_Diplomacy`'s `SDL_GetTicks()`), the config/registry reads `InitStory` makes
(`--registry none|N` overrides the registry). `st_ShowAnim` and `SaveRegistrySetting` are stubbed.

One attempt (campaign 2, difficulty 4, seed 7) stalled three times during start-up, before frame 1200 (while the
video shader was set up, before the seed is written): a start-up hang unrelated to the arguments.

## Result
The port's save stream (`SaveState::rebuild_template` → `chunks`) equals the exe's save for all five, every chunk,
with two inputs from the exe: the heap's bytes of records the original never initialises (`Game::uninit`) and the
AI's design names (`DesignPoenosanElnevez`, not ported). Findings that changed the port on the way are listed at
the top of the spec (the image as the starting state, the `WF` constructor, `StringReadAll`'s Hungarian names,
`INIT_MI`'s relations reset and its overrun into `MiTervMutato`, `NEWGAME_Research`, the map's `LoadScenario`
loading neither seeds nor relations, `FRISSIT_Planet(false, 0)`).

## Lessons
- A pre/post dump of the whole `.data` is cheap (8 MB each) and shows every global a call writes.
- Compare a start against the image, not only against the save: the pre-start dump equalled the image for the
  saved globals, which made the image a sound base.

## Scenario and MP galaxy starts (same day)
`run_newgame.py --scenario scenario/X.scn` calls `Betolt_Scenario` from `Fake_FRISSITES`; `--mp JSON` emulates the
lobby by writes (humans, AI, races, planet count) and calls `InitNewGame(seed, 2, 0, 1)`. Both take the same
before/after `.data` dumps and the exe's save at tick 0. Their seeds are wall-clock and Frida-made calls miss the
`srand` hook, so `tools/trace/scenario_seeds.py` recovers them from the post dump.

Recorded: 15 scenarios (attack espion hands intruders lightheart neighbors protect quick ring scylla snowball spying
swarm tactical tutorial) and 5 MP galaxies (20/40/60/80 planets, 0..7 AI empires, 1..3 humans, race picks).
`tests/new_game.rs` now checks all 25 starts (with the 5 campaigns) byte-exact, chunk for chunk. Findings are in
spec §13.

Lessons:
- Recordings must run one at a time: parallel ig2.exe runs corrupted or swapped the saves (empty or identical-size
  `.sav` files).
- Keep the `-post.bin` until the seeds are recovered.
