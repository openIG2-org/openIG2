# Runtime verification: the AI empire, part 1 (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/ai-empire.md`. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/ai.rs`. Replay: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/ai_trace.rs`.
Tracer: `tools/trace/run_ai.py` + `tools/trace/ai.js` (Frida 17, GOG `ig2.exe`, `scenario/quick.scn`, speed ×16).

## Method
The tracer hooks `FRISSIT_MI` (exe 0x50a6e0), which the game calls once a day, and its sub-passes, which the
exe compiles as separate functions. Every call becomes one record: the byte regions changed since the previous
record (`pre`), then a diff at each checkpoint: entering and leaving `FRISSIT_MI_SZAMOLAS` (0x50dd00), per AI
empire entering and leaving `FRISSIT_MI_GYARTAS` (0x50c6d0), `FRISSIT_MI_KEMKEDES` (0x50d0d0) and
`FRISSIT_MI_FEJLESZTES` (0x50b0b0), and the end of `FRISSIT_MI`. Regions: the AI tables (main planet/fleet,
centres, design slots, categories, colony-ship flag, sliders), danger states, money (total, pools, reserves,
shares), techs, research targets, relations, contacts and contact ages, lab levels and counts, stream states and
the weighted-choice state, spies and the mission table, spy capacities and centres, planets with their unrest,
ground strength and buildings, fleets, ships, vehicles, colony-ship pools and every empire's designs.

The replay decodes the state at a checkpoint, runs the matching segment of the port and compares **every
recorded region byte for byte** with the next checkpoint (the port writes back what it models; anything the
exe changed that the port doesn't model would show up):

| Segment | Port |
|---|---|
| `szam0 → szam1` | `AiState::bookkeeping` (centres, main fleets, contact ages) |
| previous checkpoint → `gy0` | the previous AI's sliders, then `AiState::planet_settings` |
| `gy0 → gy1` | `AiState::production` (category counts, queues, day-20 scrapping) |
| `kem0 → kem1` | `ai::spy_missions` |
| `fej0 → fej1` | `ai::research_pick` (`research::ai_pick`) |
| last checkpoint → `end` | the last sliders, then `AiState::income_shares` |

`FRISSIT_MI_KOLONIZALAS` and `FRISSIT_MI_FLOTTAK` (not ported) run between `gy1`/`kem1` and `fej0`.

`--fuzz` revives empires 1..8 as AIs (quick.scn has two living empires: AI 3 and the human 6) and, before each
call, rewrites danger states, spy records (states, missions, skill points, experience, ranks, skills), spy
capacities and centres, pool 4, relation levels and contacts, the AI's design slots, the colony-ship flag, idle
ship and vehicle lines, capacities, garrisons, ground strength, unrest, lab counts, the main planet and fleet,
sliders, and for the call only the day (20: scrapping), the difficulty (the spy roll `1/(500 − 200·Nehezseg)`),
planet 1's owner and tech 1's status (the AI's mission parameter is always 1, §6.2). `--ships` adds warships
(main fleets, ship missions, scrapping, slowing one design now and then) and hands the human's empire to the AI.

## Result
All **1,291** calls replay **byte-exact**: 1,291 bookkeeping and income-share segments, and 4,213 per-AI-empire
segments each of planet settings, production, spy missions and research picks. Coverage (port events and the
exe callees seen inside the calls): 1,104 main-fleet renames, 137 research picks, 1 ship sold on day 20 (resale
price, income, `HajoDelete`), 451 new spy missions (training 41, planet report 54, tech report 5, money 47,
sabotage 92, ship strike 22, tech theft 11, assassination 67, counter-intelligence 112), and, inside `FRISSIT_MI`, 3,141
`RndValasztas` and 1,722 `st_eSearch2spy` calls.

Not reached: rebellion missions (offered but never picked: they need planet 1 to be the target's, §6.2), MP,
`ProgramPlayers` 18, story-sale resale (`StoryAdat+0xac`).

Two tracer issues found and fixed on the way (no port changes): a third fuzzed recording replayed wrong because
the tracer re-read the state after undoing the temporary fuzz writes, so the next record's diff never carried the
undo (the replay kept planet 1's temporary owner); and fuzzed warship runs are slow (the game runs at 25 frames a
second there), so they stop at the time limit.

## Mutation pass
40 hand-made mutations of `ai.rs` (scratchpad script, one at a time, replay rerun): **28 caught**.

| Caught (mismatches) | |
|---|---|
| planet settings | no `take` decrement (259), mode 6 with ≤ 1 factories (590), labs ≥ 18 (5), aggressiveness at danger > 1 (1,222), tax at unrest > 1 (261), main planet +1 instead of +2 (29) |
| bookkeeping, sliders, shares | main-fleet ties to the last (10), no bit-5 clear (10), no contact-age decrement (1,291), danger-2 slider 49 (1,222), share step 0.02 (1,254), no overshoot fix (386) |
| production | alternative ship on ties (437), colony-ship count 2 (152), garrison < 11 (97), no danger draw (475), want ≤ (707), scrap bucket + 0.4 (4), resale ÷ 3 (2) |
| spy missions | firing at > 12 (142), unselect from 8 (19), skill ties to the second (431), weight 41 (94), money mission ÷ 3 (742), catch moment from L/4 (319), cost in f64 (299), no sabotage draw on stream 4 (347), alliance weight kept (304) |

Missed: exact boundaries the data never hits (wealth class at 75 vs 76, population 7000, experience 2000, the
main-planet margin 250 vs 251, the alliance veto at 74, the 100-credit money test, `/5000` vs `/4999`, the quota
`/100` vs `/99` at these planet counts), data-equivalent changes (the centre averaged in f64: integer
coordinates; spy score ×2001: no reordering; the 10 % scrap test at 11 %: one sale only), and one unreachable
branch: the race-5 halving of the success chance (the AI never targets race 5). These are pinned by the asm
(spec §2–§6).
