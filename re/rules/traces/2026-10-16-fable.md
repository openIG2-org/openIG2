## Result
**Exact**: all 19,965 records of the six traces (fable stories running in most of them) match the exe's story
state (buckets, queues, variables, counters, planet ids, `StoryError`, the undo list, `KemEsemeny`, the battle
pacts), stream 5 and every call the fables made, in order and with their arguments; the fleets' ids and the
`+0x18`/`+0x52` bits the stories write, the planets' radars, the human's spies, `spFlottak` and the story spy's
record (minus the exe's stack bytes) are checked against `post`. Every fable story started and ended many times
(by its own `WIN`, an abort or a staged `WIN`); the port emitted, among others, `FleetFlags`, `FleetState`,
`SetFleetId`, `SetPlanetRadar`, `ShowWormhole`, `StorySpy`/`SpyReport`, `KillSpy`, `JoinBattle`, `ScrollText`,
`WithdrawMessage` and `send_away`'s orders, each matched by the exe. The earlier campaign, storyline, MP, BRnd,
Begin, Rnd and Elem traces still replay exactly with `FableStories` in the script list.

Found on the way:
- The exe drops every `st_oldLetilt` money check of the ELF (305, 309, 312 never disable an answer).
- 303 restores the escorted merchant's id with an inlined read that leaves `StoryError` alone.
- 311 joins the human's fleets by looping the fleet records up to the **ship** count, reading stale records past
  `OsszHanyFlotta` (the tracer dumps them now).
- The fables' pirates make real-time battles with the human; on the battle screen the frame hook isn't called, so
  staged calls queued up and a first set of fake participant lists was overwritten before its call (now written at
  the call, and two-sided battles are auto-resolved while recording).

## Mutation pass
A first pass on the superseded traces 61..66: 54 single-expression mutations of `fable.rs` (scratchpad script, one
replay each), 34 killed. The survivors are boundaries or paths the fuzzer doesn't reach: 301's distance of exactly
60 and its tie rule, 301's exclusion of the participant's own slot (it never won a draw), `st_fGetNearest`'s and
303's ties and the 79/80 limit, the merchants' id range edge 11000, the truncation of 303's target, the inlined

trader's bit set, the daily owner change), `GetBarmilyenBirodalom`'s step `e mod 8 + 1` (no miss reached empire 8),
310's spy flag (cleared at the end of the check; kind 2 never matches the death test), the agent's `+0x16` test
and spy 0 in the daily scan, 311's `< 4` planets, its loop bound (ship count against fleet count: both cover the
fleets the traces had) and the pirate-only branch, 315's lab level 0, 316's 10-draw limit. A second pass on the
traces 71..77 caught 310's XP range and 311's join distance; it left 301's distance of exactly 60, 303's of exactly
80, `st_fEliminate`'s side test at exactly 50 and `st_fDistFromBir`'s skipped last planet. All follow the exe's
asm (§8.15).
