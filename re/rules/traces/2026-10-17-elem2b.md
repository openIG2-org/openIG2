## Tracer lessons
- `BolygoElfoglal` (exe 0x53b720) posts `Story_Special(0xB, EN, e)` for an empire the human sees but doesn't know
  **before** it marks it known: inside 808's end (flag 4 still set) the nested `Story_Check` runs 808's end again, and
  the recursion runs until the stack is gone (an original bug; in the game the human knows the empire it just fought).
  The staging marks the empire known for the record.
- A real `BolygoElfoglal(p, EN)` staged before 808's end made the campaign reset itself; the staging writes the owner
  byte for the record instead. Madness left on a planet after a staged battle start makes it count as at war: the
  staging unmarks it after the record, and 808's start's `StoryAdat+0x40`/`+0x88` are cleared after it (in the game the
  battle follows).
- `TankNew(−p, …)` and `st_GetTerv` fault for an owner without a design table (a map's inactive empire, free planets):
  the staging picks owners with designs; the generic elem staging no longer starts 808/854.
- `--debug` now reports faults (the exe VA), the game's deaths, wins and `ExitToMainScreen`, streams the calls of a
  staged battle end, and the recorder stops after 150 s without records; the `OutputDebugString` exceptions (the
  game's log lines, `RaiseException` 0x40010006/0x4001000a) are skipped. Campaign 2 with seed 232 resets itself at
  the same moment every run (not used).
