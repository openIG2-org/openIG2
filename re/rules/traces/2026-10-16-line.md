## Result
All 54,313 campaign records (the 9 Line traces and the 8 older ones, which keep `ScenarioStories` alone) replay
exactly: story state, stream 5, `MEGNYERES`/`MEGHALAS` counts and every call the stories made, in order and with
their arguments. In records where a Line story was running the exe made 1,235 `NewMessage`, 191 `SetParamStr`, 90
`KiszedUzenet`, 82 `LetiltReszlet`, 34 `BolygoInfoRemove`, 239 `st_bUnhide`, 211 conquests and 45 colonisations,
68 free buildings, 150 money changes and 7 `FelfedezBirodalom` calls; every timer of the eight stories ran out at
least once (by the game's tick or staged).

Found on the way:
- `NewMessage` can slow the game down (`SetIdoLelassit`); `FRISSIT_Story`'s counter loop re-reads
  `IdoSpeedSzorzo`, so the counters after a message-sending one run down by the new speed in the same tick (the
  replay takes the speed after the call from the record; the port reads it live from the world).
- Story 405's message (inline, ported earlier) also sets parameter 1 to the sender's name, between `NewMessage`
  and the race override: now seen because `SetParamStr` is logged.
- `st_GetBirodalom808hoz` in the exe tests `Viszony` level > 2 where the ELF calls `st_HaborubanAllE`; 852's is the
  same function. Story 550 restarts **530's** counter 5310 when the human doesn't know empires 1 and 5 (kept).
- `st_fNewEx` mode 1 reads owner 0's radar row when the human has no planet left (planet slot 0).

## Mutation pass
56 single-expression mutations of `storyline.rs` and its helpers (scratchpad script, one replay each): 52 killed.
Survivors, not pinned by the traces: the `Story_SkipFirst == 0` start guard of 530 and 560 (the fuzzer sets the
flag only around 550, whose guard and `== 8` ending are pinned), `near()`'s `<` against `<=` (an exact distance
tie), the truncation of the start planet's coordinates (the campaign maps' coordinates are whole numbers), and the
gamble's `< 50` against `< 49` (needs a draw of exactly 49). They follow the exe's asm (§8.9).
