# Ground battle AI (`WF::MI`): runtime verification (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/ground-ai.md`. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/ground/ai.rs` (`War::mi`, `War::ai_forget`).
Replay: `tests/ground_rt_trace.rs::ground_ai_matches_trace`. Tracer: `tools/trace/run_ground_rt.py --mode battle`
+ `ground_rt.js` (Frida 17, GOG `ig2.exe`, `scenario/quick.scn`).

## Method
The real-time tracer stages AI-against-AI ground wars on the colony screen and hooks the game's own
`FRISSIT_Grwar` → `WF::MI` (exe 0x486d40) → `WF::Refresh` every 20 Hz tick, recording byte-region diffs
around each call (battlefield `WF(p)`, every unit record with hp/origin/waypoints, projectile lists, planet,
buildings, streams) and a pinned libc pair. The replay rebuilds the state before each `"mi"` event, runs
`War::mi(speed)` and compares the 18 AI blocks (`WF+0x33c+0x40e`), every unit (`tick_key`: orders, flags,
groups `+0x248`, homes `+0x24c`, hit markers `+0x25c/+0x260`, targets, movement state), the air-strike
charges, the fighter flag `WF+0x32c`, streams 2 and 13 and the libc draws.

Plain battles mostly showed strategies 1, 3 and 6; `--ai-fuzz` (new) perturbs, before about one call in N,
the AI blocks (strategy 0..=6 with 0 = choose again, phase, mode, timer, counter), a few hit markers and
attackers, and arms a random AI unit with a fighter or big-ship strike (weapons 0x20/8, `+0x288`, charge
200/300). The writes happen before the `pre` read, so the trace carries them.

| Trace | Mode | `WF::MI` calls |
|---|---|---|
| `grt-battle-*`, `grt-small-*` (real-time ground war recordings) | plain, fuzzed, human, end/destruct | 8,527 |
| `grt-ai-61` | `--ai-fuzz` (1 in 25) | 429 |

## Findings while verifying
- `ShotSpecWeapon`'s stream-2 cost: 102 draws for big ships (the bomb constructor exe 0x457ad0 draws once more
  per bomb, plus one libc `rand()` on screen), 7 for fighters (`AddFighters` 0x45b8d0: five, only when no wave
  is up). The spec's first estimate (82 / 2) was the spread alone.
- A never-set move destination is uninitialised memory (e.g. 1781186419, 930819277): the "moving" test squares
  in wrapping i32, sqrt of the negative sum is NaN and `cvttss2si` gives `INT_MIN`, so the unit doesn't count
  as moving. Found by `--ai-fuzz` (12 mismatches before the fix).
- `GetMITarget`'s over-read is real and unreproducible: hp arrays are `realloc`ed to exactly `size` ints. In the
  fuzzed battle `grt-battle-fuzz-51`, groups of 3 choose among single-vehicle turrets and pick farther ones
  than the in-bounds formula; the port reads 0 past the end. The replay classifies a mismatch as heap only
  when the unit's target differs and some live enemy group is smaller than it.

## Mutations
11 of 12 caught: the attacker's 2 → 3 remap, strategy 3's 5/7 steps, the Chebyshev maximum, the in-range /3,
the 2-cell line spacing, the attacker reset in the marker walk, the wrapping moving test, the pincer angle,
the fortress coin, the timer's speed decrement, the overwhelm rule. Missed: the air-strike scan's own-unit
penalty ×3 → ×2 (no recorded strike's best cell changes; asm-pinned, `mulss` by the f32 3.0 at 0x848544).
