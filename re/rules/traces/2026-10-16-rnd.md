## Result
**Exact**: all 50,872 records match the exe's story state, stream 5 and every outermost call the stories made, in
order with their arguments: 2,970 messages (every id of the 15 stories, campaign 3's own ids too, 118's fifth-count
0x4a7 included), 2,002 text and 1,592 number parameters, 595 payments (118's bounties, 170's double payment of the
reminder, the tributes, 172's −9 slip, 242's hire and yearly fees), 217 techs given, 245 messages taken back, 293
planet infos added and 216 removed, 144 epidemics, 320 hero fleets with their ship and order, 316 ship deletions,
108 videos; 29 space battles changed 242's bonuses. The earlier campaign traces (77,032 records) still replay
exactly.

## What the replay found
- `Virus_CheckAdd` (exe 0x542930) is not only the info: it sets `StoryAdat+0x30 := 1` (the epidemic switch, §4 step 3).
- The epidemic switch's daily end test reads fleets' `+0x52` bit 2: the test world needs fleets' flags (a fleet of
  the human's carried the epidemic in campaign 2).
- 170's reminder answer pays twice: the price, then `min(price, (int) MoneyMennyi)` read after the first payment.
- `st_GetRandomTalalmany(−1, 0)` resolves −1 to `EN` (exe 0x5eab60 `cmove`).
