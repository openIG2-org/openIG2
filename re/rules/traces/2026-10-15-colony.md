# Colony economy: runtime verification (2026-10-15)

Spec: `../colony-economy.md`. Target: GOG `ig2.exe` (sha256 2baa530b…0442), `FRISSIT_Planet` @ 0x474930.

## Method
`tools/trace/run_colony.py` (Frida 17, `_local/venv`) spawns the exe, loads `scenario/quick.scn` at
speed 3 and hooks `FRISSIT_Planet`. For every outer economy call `FRISSIT_Planet(true, 0)` the agent
(`tools/trace/colony.js`) records:
- before and after: all owned planets (raw 0x188-byte records), their building arrays, the 21 RNG
  streams, `GlobalisMoral`, `BolygoNeededMoral`, `BolygoMennyiIdejeLazad`, `FullGDP`/`FullTax`,
  `StoryAdat`, `CsaladtagElrabolvaKi`, the live `ProblemakLeiro`, player/race tables;
- every `rndlog` call made inside it (stream, range, source line, result) and every nested refresh.

`--fuzz` additionally randomises the live state before each call with a fixed-seed LCG: tax levels
0–7, population, morale, epidemics, spies, morale penalties, unrest, event codes, building damage,
yield %, switched-off flags and some building types (guns, self-destruct, water plant, stadium,
production improvement). The exe then computes the outcome of states quick.scn never reaches.

`engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/colony_trace.rs` decodes each `before`, runs `colony::economy_step` with
rule tables read from the user's exe, and compares every modelled planet field, every building slot,
the needed morale, the unrest counter, the monthly empire totals and the stream-3 state with `after`.
The traces stay in `_local/traces/` (game state; not committed).

Fuzz coverage: all tax levels, morale 0–989, 327 epidemic planet-steps, 387 with spies, 1,599 buildings
without energy, 113 without workers, 3,150 damaged, 1,816 switched off, guns and self-destruct
buildings, event codes, unrest > 0, building completions (nested refreshes).

## Findings from the runtime check
1. **Event slots are 1..=5.** The first spec said `BolygoInfoIs` scans six slots from `+0x168`. The fuzz
   wrote codes into `+0x168` and the game ignored them (e.g. the morale cap −0x1F7C and the population
   freeze −0x1F4A had no effect). The exe (`add eax, 0xb86012`, loop 1..=5) and the ELF (`local_14 = 1`)
   both start at slot 1. Fixed in spec and code.
2. **A rebellion happens mid-step.** In fuzz step 38, planet 46 crossed 30 unrest: `BolygoFellazad`
   rolled on streams 1 and 8, rewrote the planet (owner 6 → 3, tax, morale, epidemic cleared), and the
   economy continued on the new state (no epidemic roll). Until `BolygoFellazad` is ported, the test
   compares only the planets before the first rebel of a step.
3. **Precision.** Mutating the port to the ELF's f64 `ln`, or to an f32 `0.66` comparison, still passes:
   those exe-specific roundings are not observable in these traces (morale is coarse). They are kept
   on instruction-level evidence (spec §10). Logic mutations are caught: tax ×4 instead of ×5
   (643 mismatches), build speed 49 instead of 50 (218), clamp roll range off by one (105).
4. The live `ProblemakLeiro` equals the exe file at every call, except entry 4's rate/weight, which
   the game rewrites per planet (spec §5.3).

## Not covered
Multiplayer (morale unclamped, no penalties), story hooks (`StoryAdat+0x20/+0x24/+0x30`; the
`StoryAdat+0x2c` food-loss total is updated but not compared), autobuild purchases, the yearly roll-over.
