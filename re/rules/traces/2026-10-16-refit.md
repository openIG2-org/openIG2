# Building refit at scenario load (`EpuleteketJoHelyreTesz`) and the vehicle pass (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/colony-placement.md` §1, §6. Port: `Game::refit_buildings` (`ig2-sim::game`), built from the verified
`EpuletDelete` (`ground::War::delete_building`), `LocateSuitableLocation` (`placement::PlacementRules::locate`) and
`EpuletNew` (`colony_side::ColonyWorld::new_building` + `War::{add_building_turrets, building_built}`);
`ground::Field::placed_cells` / `SaveState::vehicle_cells` give the search the ground units on the map, now also in
the economy step (autobuild, AI orders) and colonisation arrivals. Test: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/save_replay.rs`
`building_refit_matches_the_exe`. Tracer: `tools/trace/run_save.py --refit` + `save.js` `fuzzBuildings()`.

## Evidence

ELF (-O0) `EpuleteketJoHelyreTesz` 0x61cd10, called from `LoadScenario` 0x64b63b and `ExecuteString` 0x57e183. Its
exe equivalent is **0x53cd00** (found as the third caller of `LocateSuitableLocation` 0x4869d0, besides
`EpuletNewSearchLocation` 0x53cca0 and the story helper 0x5c95b0), called unconditionally at the end of the exe's
`LoadScenario` (0x552d8f, after the spies' state fix-up) and from `ExecuteString` (0x4ddb7c). The two match step for
step: owned planets (`+0x20`), slots re-reading `HanyEpuletLefoglalva`, `(int)` positions, `EpuletDelete` 0x53c730,
`LocateSuitableLocation` with the old cell as start (`*x ≥ 0`), `EpuletNew` 0x53c7d0 with `done = 1`, damage 0.
`LocateSuitableLocation`'s vehicle pass (exe 0x486a77..0x486ae5): `WF[p]` count `+0x180` (0x9c33c4), used flags
pointer `+0x188`, unit pointers `+0x330`; a unit with neither `+0x188` bit 1 nor bit 30 and without `+0x18c` bit 2
blocks its cell `(+0x1a0, +0x1a4)` as a ringless 1 × 1 footprint: the same filter as `LocateTankSuitablePosition`'s.

## Recording

`run_save.py --refit` on `scenario/quick.scn`: at each listed day the agent perturbs, on every owned planet, a quarter
of the buildings' positions (whole and quarter cells, a fifth of them near the map edges), a twelfth onto another
building's cell, damage and remaining work on a quarter, empties a twelfth of the slots with the exe's own
`EpuletDelete` (holes the refit fills), and moves half of the battlefield units onto building cells (± 2); then writes
a save (`LoadSave(slot, name, 2)`), calls 0x53cd00 from the frame hook and writes a second save. The port loads the
first save, runs `Game::saved()` (the write's epilogue) and `Game::refit_buildings()`, and must write the second
(every modelled chunk group: buildings, counts, battlefields with their turrets).

Recordings in `_local/traces/refit/` (`<tag>-<2k>.sav` before, `<tag>-<2k+1>.sav` after; never committed).

## Result

All 10 refits byte-exact: 2,060 buildings re-placed, 569 of them at a new cell or slot. The full test suite still
passes with the vehicle pass wired into the economy step and colonisation (the save replays and colony-side traces had
no unit on a searched cell).

Mutations (each must fail): the vehicle pass dropped → 8–18 chunk groups differ per refit; the start hint dropped
(centroid start) → ~280; no `BuildingBuilt` → ~60; the old damage kept → ~10. All four caught.
