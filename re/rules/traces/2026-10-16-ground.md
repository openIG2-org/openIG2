# Ground combat (strategic side): runtime verification (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/ground-combat.md`. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/ground.rs`. Replay: `tests/ground_trace.rs`.
Tracer: `tools/trace/run_ground.py` + `ground.js` (Frida 17, GOG `ig2.exe`, `scenario/quick.scn`).

## Method
Ground wars don't happen on their own in `quick.scn` within minutes, so the agent stages them from the
per-frame hook with the exe's own functions:
- **`war`**: picks a planet of an empire 1..=8 and another empire with ground designs, creates a fleet next to
  the planet (`FlottaNew`), maybe a carrier ship (`HajoNew`), 1–3 batches of ground vehicles (`TankNew`) and
  sometimes extra garrison vehicles on the planet (`TankNew(−p, …)`, which lands them on the battlefield with
  the exe's own `AddTank`/`RearrangeTankGroups`). Then `srand(S1)`, `AddTanksToPlanet(f, p, 1)`, two `rand()`;
  `srand(S2)`, `WF::Simulate` (thiscall on `WF(p)`), two `rand()`. Snapshots before, between and after: every
  unit group's 0x290-byte record with its hit-point and origin arrays, the battlefield globals, `Tankok`, all
  fleets, the planet with its buildings, the RNG streams, `timing`. The libc draw count is found in the
  replay by stepping the MSVC LCG from `S` to the recorded pair. `--fuzz` gives the designs random ground
  pods (paralysers, manipulators, kamikazes, air strikes, stealth, radar, regeneration, …), flips building
  "working"/"switched off" bits and damage, makes the attacker human-controlled (its units keep flag 2),
  and sets the story damage bonus.
- **`indit`**: the same staging, then `GroundWarIndit(f, p)` with random human-controlled sides and debug
  switches, `StartGrWar` patched to `ret`. The route is read from its effects (`timing` changed = simulated,
  `GrWar_MultiCsata_bolygo == p` = real-time); a real-time landing is undone with
  `GroundWarFinished(p, owner, 1)`.

## Result
**324 wars and 150 routings exact.** Every alive group's design, owner, side, members' hit points and
origins, flags, flags2, weapons, pods, ammunition, regeneration, air units, gun bit, hull, size marker,
partner/carrier links, paralysis and seizure countdowns, building slot, target, index and aim; the dead
slots; the battlefield's attacking fleet, countdown, status cache and presence tables; the vehicle table;
every fleet's sums and in-battle bit; the planet's owner, garrison, strength, war flag, building count, each
building's type and damage; stream 2; `timing`; and the number of libc `rand()` draws (136,217).

Covered by the 324 wars: 4,597 passes, 3,759 groups destroyed, 73 buildings fell (turrets), gun shots
W4 47,542 / W5 12,795 / W0 1,932 / W3 491 / W7 430 / W1 308 / W8 293 / W6 135 / W9 124, 214 paralyses,
56 seizures, 3 kamikazes, 6 fighter strikes, 2,584 one-vehicle splits, 42 joins (the 20-group limit).
Routing: 111 real-time, 39 simulated, 2 captures (37 battlefields compared after a full `GroundWarIndit`,
including `GroundWarSetBattleStatus`'s status refresh).

Findings:
- **Original crash** (spec §8): with transporter pods (0x9f) the exe faulted twice inside `WF::Simulate`
  (`ground-war-fuzz-7a` war 24, `ground-war-fuzz-99a` war 2): a group split off a partner group copies the
  partner flag but its carrier index is uninitialised memory; when it dies the exe writes through it. The
  agent now records each fuzzed war's input first (`pre`); the port finishes the recorded one
  (`ground_war_survives_original_crashes`).
  The fuzzer now uses the transporter pod rarely.
- **Hang hazard** (spec §5.1): the first `indit` session stalled. `GroundWarSetBattleStatus` started the
  end-of-battle countdown on a human planet whose field the agent had cleaned by hand (`last status` was
  −1); the countdown only runs down on the colony screen, so `GetStatus` stayed "contested" and the next
  simulated war there looped forever. The agent now sets `last status := owner` after its clean-up.
- `Story_GroundBattleSimulateCheck` clears the story ban before deciding (the fuzzed pre-call value is
  irrelevant): spec §2 step 4 corrected.
- The exe's `WF::Simulate` differs from the ELF's (asm-checked, all confirmed by the replay): the attacker
  index passed to `Damage` is 0 (a `push 0` left on the stack for the callee), the target refresh rule, the
  inlined second-enemy search, `GroundWarFinished(…, true)` via a `push 1`.

Not traced: MP, the story hold, mines (unreachable in the auto-resolve), transporter partners taking hits,
the 0x10 ammunition weapon (hull index 4 with special pods), big-ship strikes, the stealth dodge, captures'
colony side (`BolygoElfoglal`, verified in `2026-10-15-colony-side.md`), positions (`LocateTankSuitablePosition`
is a hook; positions only matter to mines and the real-time battle).

## Mutation pass
30 single-point mutations of `ground.rs`; **18 caught** (attrition, the ×1.2 bonus, paralysis/seizure
rolls and durations, the stealth dodge and the NJ stealth test, carry-over (the mutant loops forever), split
hit points, the 20-group limit, equalising, the story bonus, re-embark room, the voice draw, air strikes'
flag-2 test, pods as a maximum, the ammunition/air reset, rearranging the previous planet's owner). Missed:
- Equivalent in these states: the 10-pass status break (every pass ends early through the attrition's
  `any`, and every `Damage` clears the status cache anyway); `JoinGroups`' size-marker reset (the end of
  `RearrangeTankGroups` resets every marker); the draw order in `WF_Tank::Damage` (`Damage` never reaches
  a dead group); `FirstFreeC`'s `hp < 1` (dead members are always removed at once).
- Boundaries the traces never hit: the kamikaze roll at exactly 50, building damage exactly equal to its
  hit points, a fleet 19–20 units away at re-embarking.
- States not reached: regeneration pods (0x9e) on a member below half its hit points, the fallback to
  paralysed targets, groups with mixed origins (the stale-origin read), a unit aiming at the index equal to
  a fallen building's slot, a kamikaze's secondary hit killing.
They are pinned by the asm (spec references).
