# Colonisation and terraforming arrivals: runtime verification (2026-10-15)

Spec: `../fleet-movement.md` §4.3 cases 1–2, `../colony-side.md` §5–5.1. Target: GOG `ig2.exe`
(sha256 2baa530b…0442), `FRISSIT_Starmap` @ 0x594ae0 (arrival branches 0x59649d..0x5968fa).

## Method
`tools/trace/run_colony_side.py --mode arrive` (agent `colony_side.js`): on `FRISSIT_Starmap` calls that
aren't radar refreshes, 1–2 fleets get a live AI owner, the colonising (bit3) or terraforming (bit4) flag,
a colony ship/terraformer and a move to an unowned planet, and are placed on its approach point
(x, y + 2) so they arrive in that call's movement loop. Half of the two-fleet ticks send both fleets to
one planet with the colonist on the lower fleet index. Full colony snapshots (planets with buildings,
tech table, contacts, fleets, seeds, the `NextColonyMap` cursor 0x996420) are taken around the real call;
the static part records `Terepek[i]+0` for every terrain.

`engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/colony_side_trace.rs` (`fleet_arrivals_match_the_game`) builds a `Starmap`
from the snapshot's raw fleet records (`Fleet::from_record`) and runs the real `Starmap::tick` with
`ColonyArrivals` (the colony world, the colony-map search, terraforming). It compares every staged planet
record (buildings included), all planet owners, stream 1, the map cursor, `BirodalomIsmeri`, and whether
each staged fleet was used up. The live terrain types equal `parse_surfaces` (75 maps).

Mutations: affinity row by race instead of owner number (102 mismatches), last instead of first maximum
(102), map cursor ignored (80), starmap view of a colonised planet not updated mid-loop (22). Not caught:
the cursor wrap after the last map of a kind (no kind ran out in 43 terraformings); asm only.

## Findings
1. Terraforming picks the surface from `FajBolygoViszony[owner]`: the owner's **empire number** is the
   row, not its race (`lea (owner, owner, 8)` @ 0x59658e).
2. `NextColonyMap` (0x486920) is a per-kind round-robin and never returns map 60 (→ 63).
3. Production at the end of the tick may build a new fleet into the colonist's freed slot (the last free
   one): the replay treats a record without the colonist's flags at another position as a reuse.
4. The terraform branch pumps frames (`Fake_FRISSITES` with 0xb79f78 = 1) while rebuilding star
   sprites: UI only, nothing in the traced state changes.

## Not covered
The human as the arriving owner (story/UI messages), multiplayer, colonisation of planets with ground
units, `NextColonyMap` for a kind with no map (returns the terrain count).
