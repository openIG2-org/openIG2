# 2026-10-16: the story engine and the scenario stories, replayed against ig2.exe

Spec: `re/rules/campaign.md`. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/story.rs`. Replay: `tests/story_trace.rs`.
Tracer: `tools/trace/run_story.py` + `story.js`.

## What was recorded
The tracer spawns the GOG `ig2.exe`, loads a skirmish scenario with `Betolt_Scenario` from the frame hook and
records:
- **tick**: every `FRISSIT_Story` (exe 0x5c1ff0) the game runs, with the whole story state (buckets, queues,
  tables, event flags and payloads, `StoryAdat`, the undo list) and the world inputs of the scenario stories
  (planet owners, `+0x28`, the human's planet visibility, fleet owners and ids, `BirodalomEl`,
  `BirodalomHanyBolygo`, the human's money, its relation levels, one tech row, stream 5) before and after;
- **check**: every outermost `Story_Check` (0x5c5610) the game enters from a hook (`Story_FleetArrivedCheck`
  return 0x5c8ca0: 71, `Story_KemEsemeny` 0x5c9091: 4, `Story_Special` 0x5c95a1: 21);
- **staged** (`--fuzz`): calls the tracer makes from the frame hook between the game's ticks: `StartStory` +
  `Story_Check(1)`, `Story_Special` with random data, a fleet arrival (`Story_FleetArrivedSave` +
  `…Check` with the fleet's order set to a point, a planet or a fleet), `Story_SpaceBattleFinished` with a fake
  participant list, raw event flags + `Story_Check(1)` (space battle start, counter, daily, signal, …),
  `st_AddGameInfo`, `st_RemoveGameInfo`, `WIN`.
Between records the fuzzer queues the scenario stories 360..375 with random variables (and exact boundary
values: money, planet counts, planet `+0x28`), planet ids and fleet ids (live and dead fleet slots), counters
and unrelated variables/planet ids. `MEGNYERES` (0x53f6b0) and `MEGHALAS` (0x53b630) are patched to count
their calls (and keep the last argument) and return, so won and lost scenarios go on.

## Result
All **27,006 records exact**: the whole story state, stream 5 and the `MEGNYERES`/`MEGHALAS` calls (count and
argument) after each record equal the exe's. Coverage: 10,347 ticks, 96 hook checks, 16,563 staged calls; 1,405
counters ran out, 1,833 daily checks; every scenario story started (3,388 starts) and ended (3,243), 1,341
victories, 3,542 deaths of the human, 2,406 info lines (story 371's countdown and staged ones), 881 stream-5
draws (`st_bSearch(−e, 1)` in story 375 and `st_fSearch`).

Findings on the way (spec'd, kept):
- The "daily" check is not daily: it runs when `Ido` is **more than** 50 past the last one, every 51 ticks at
  ×1, 13 at ×4, 4 at ×16. Only the ×1 trace tells `<` from `≤` (at ×4/×16 `Ido` never lands on +50).
- A skirmish keeps only the scenario stories in the buckets; the campaign scripts and `st_Multi_include` run
  every check but find nothing to do.
- The exe drops the scripts' asserts: several start blocks are empty; story 373 reads its variables in the
  opposite order to the ELF (visible in `StoryError`).
- `WIN` on a story that isn't running still queues a finish signal; a failed search makes the scripts read
  planet/fleet slot 0 (the tracer records slot 0 for that).

- Unreachable in a skirmish (no script or hook puts the state there): `Remove` matching `−id` (no negative
  story ids), the specials queue's `data1` (`TalalmanyVodor` stays empty), the signal flag in the nested check
  (no scenario story reads flag 12), story 361 skipping empire 0 (`BirodalomEl[0]` is always 0).
- Boundary-only in the data: story 367's `<` (the money is never exactly the threshold), 374's level 5 (no NAP
  with the human in these scenarios), `st_bSearch` taking the `r+1`-th planet (only matters when `r` is the
  last candidate).
- `StoryError` details (`st_szGet`, `st_vSet` and story 373's read order): the final value is set by later
  accessors in the same check; no script reads it.
- Story 371's "years" text: the info line's text isn't recorded (UI); its id and the undo entry are.
