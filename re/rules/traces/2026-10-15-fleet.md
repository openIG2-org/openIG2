# Fleet movement: runtime verification (2026-10-15)

Spec: `../fleet-movement.md`. Target: GOG `ig2.exe` (sha256 2baa530b…0442), `FRISSIT_Starmap` @ 0x594ae0.

## Method
`tools/trace/run_fleet.py` (Frida 17, `_local/venv`) spawns the exe, loads `scenario/quick.scn` and hooks
`FRISSIT_Starmap`. For each sim tick the agent (`tools/trace/fleet.js`) records before and after: every
fleet slot (raw 0x80 bytes, freed slots included), the waypoint pool, wormholes, the RNG streams,
`TradeIncomeThisYear` and globals; plus planet positions/owners/epidemics and finished trade
buildings, the relation matrix, and on speed-refresh ticks (`Ido2 % 10 == 1`) the ship table with the
designs it uses. Every `rndlog` inside the call is logged.

`--fuzz` (fixed-seed LCG, every 40 ticks): random move orders to nearby points, planets and other
fleets, waypoint chains, patrol routes, speed overrides, and trade centres/banks/ports placed on
random planets. Runs mostly at speed 3 (`IdoSpeedSzorzo` 16).

`engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/fleet_trace.rs` decodes each `before`, takes the radar pass's outputs
(visibility, radar range, in-battle and stealth bits; not ported) from `after`, runs `Starmap::tick`
and compares every modelled fleet field bit-for-bit (positions as f32 bits), the waypoint pool, the
wormholes, stream 14 and the trade income. Traces stay in `_local/traces/` (game state).

moves, 593 arrivals, 1,808 rolls, 19 trader payments, all exact, with §6 now computed instead of copied.

## Findings from the runtime check
1. **Double constants.** With `step = speed × 0.3f` the port drifted by 1 ulp on fleets at the 0.06
   crawl speed. The exe computes `(f32)((f64)speed × 0.3)`, `(f32)((f64)step + 0.003)` and compares
   `(f64)dd > 0.0001` (`mulsd [0x852248]`, `addsd [0x852338]`, `comisd [0x848520]`). Fixed.
2. **Freed slots are still read.** A fleet following a fleet that was just deleted stops; the game then
   evaluates "contact lost" on the freed slot's stale record. The first capture dumped only live slots
   and the replay disagreed; the agent now dumps all slots.
3. **Merge renames.** A merge recomputes `+0x52` bit11 from the fleet name (`FlottaNevLatszik`: name ≠
   localised default "Fleet"); names are now part of the model.
4. Mutations caught: f32 `0.3` (16 mismatches), patrol node not refreshed (8), no lead halving (401),
   trade-port weight ×2 instead of ×3 (6).

## Not covered
Battles and planet attacks (none occurred; the test would skip such ticks), wormholes (none in
quick.scn), colonisation/terraforming arrivals, virus carriers, multiplayer paths, the debug-speed and
"all fleets visible" switches.
