# Runtime verification: the AI empire, part 2 (2026-10-16)

Spec: `re/rules/ai-empire.md` §8–§13. Port: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/src/ai/` (`colonise.rs`, `fleets.rs`, `design.rs`,
`units.rs`). Replay: `engine/crates/ig2-sim/tests/ai_trace.rs`. Tracer: `tools/trace/run_ai.py` + `tools/trace/ai.js`
(Frida 17, GOG `ig2.exe`, `scenario/quick.scn`, speed ×16).

## Method
Part 1's tracer (`traces/2026-10-16-ai-empire.md`) gains checkpoints entering and leaving
`FRISSIT_MI_KOLONIZALAS` (exe 0x50d9a0), `FRISSIT_MI_FLOTTAK` (0x50b1c0), `FRISSIT_MI_DESIGN` (0x50abf0, nested in
the other two) and the war declaration `ElkuldHaduzenet` (0x4c09c0, nested in the fleet pass), and new regions:
the AI's day counters, the last fled colony target, the scouting grid, diplomacy's power and planet counts, the
may-attack flags, fortification strength, the live radar tables, the fleet name counters, the story's spare-the-human
flags and the waypoint pool (fleets, ships, vehicles, planets with garrisons, stations and queues, and every
empire's designs were recorded already).

| Segment | Port |
|---|---|
| `kol0 → kol1` | `AiState::colonise` (with any nested design pass) |
| `flo0 → flo1` | `AiState::fleets` (with any nested design pass and war declaration) |
| `des0 → des1` | `AiState::design` |

Nested segments are checked on their own and as part of the enclosing one. Every recorded region must match the
closing checkpoint byte for byte. The war declaration is diplomacy's (`diplomacy.md` §5, verified by its traces):
the replay's hook applies the exe's recorded changes to relations, contacts and contact ages at the moment the port
declares, and the port must declare the same wars in the same order. Localised names (a new design's, the main
fleet's) and the bytes of records appended to grown arrays (new fleet slots, new design records: uninitialised
memory in the original) are taken from the exe.

Fuzzing for part 2 (`--fuzz`, on top of part 1's): the day counters (the design upgrade timer runs out: the
`FRISSIT_MI_DESIGN(e, true)` path), the last fled colony target, scouting-grid cells, the may-attack flags, contact
ages (negative: war declarations allowed), free planets put in sight, colony fleets of 1–3 ships, ground vehicles on
the AI's planets and in its main fleet, transports in the main fleet, foreign fleets moved next to the AI's fleets and
next to free planets (visible to everyone), fleet speeds, AIs at peace (no war: a declaration is possible), and for
one call only: an AI without planets (`BirodalomHanyBolygo` 0: it declares war on any target in sight) and an AI with
nothing left to colonise (≥ 6 planets, every free planet hidden: its colony-ship designs are deleted and redone).
The design slots are fuzzed with live designs of the slot's own hull (arbitrary records crash `TervAutoDesign` /
`TervUjraSzamol` once the upgrade timer runs out). The story's guards (`StoryAdat+0xb0/+0xb8/+0xbc`) are set only for
the duration of `FRISSIT_MI_FLOTTAK` (their only reader) and recorded in its checkpoint: left set beyond it, the
story check reset the game (the clock read 0 and the game left the scenario). All recordings use `--ships` (warships,
and the human's empire handed to the AI: otherwise the human's diplomacy screens stop the clock).

## Result
All **361** new calls replay **byte-exact** together with part 1's 1,291 (1,652 in all): **1,444** colonisation
passes, **1,444** fleet passes and **351** design passes (293 `FRISSIT_MI_DESIGN(e, true)` from the upgrade timer,
58 `(e, false)` after the colony-ship designs went), 29 war declarations at the same points as the exe. Coverage
inside the calls (exe callees): 574 colony ships sent (`DeleteKolonizalo`), 188 new fleets, 22,081
`MenekulesHaKell` checks (260 regroups, 231 flights, 510 close flights), 48 vehicle transfers (`TankAtpakol`, loading
and unloading), 112 `TervDelete`s (with part 1's sales: 180 `HajoDelete`s and 118 `FlottaDelete`s), 72 `TervNew`s and 7,006 `TervAutoDesign`s;
the fleet orders the pass changed: 554 moves to a point (fleeing, scouting), 784 follow orders (regrouping, joining
the main fleet), 5 moves to a planet, 73 attacks on planets, 75 attacks on fleets.

Not reached: the merge back of a colony fleet on a dangerous route (`FlottaOsszevon` in §9: needs a multi-ship
colony fleet sent to a free planet with an enemy fleet beside it; staged without success, see the fuzz list; a unit
test covers it), MP, `ProgramPlayers` 18, the tutorial event, `TalalmanyRarakhato`'s debug switch, the original's
endless unloading loop (§11.6).

Tracer issues found on the way (no port changes): the fuzz first put arbitrary designs into the slots, which made
the exe's own auto-designer crash; the story flags written for a whole day made the game leave the scenario (the
tracer looked hung: its frame hook stopped, a second Frida script sampling the thread stacks showed the game in its
menu and video code); overlapping temporary writes need undoing newest first.

## Mutation pass
23 hand-made mutations of the part-2 port (scratchpad script, one at a time, replay rerun): **13 caught**.

| Caught (mismatches) | |
|---|---|
| fleets | speed class + 0.4 (29), danger reach 1.0 → 1.1 (209), flee angle weight 0.125 → 0.25 (24), safety slope 6 → 5 (15), scouting weight /2 (1), loading phase 299 (6), no-load 61 after unloading (7) |
| colonisation | nearest colony fleet metric (4) |
| design | auto-design ties to the first tech (1,066), upgrade block on the wrong day (14) |
| units | `TervDelete`'s last queue word (3), `FlottaNew` default speed (194) |

Missed: precision changes the traced values never expose (`MenekulesSuly`'s far branch in f32, the colonisation
factor in f32: both pinned by the asm), branches and boundaries the data never hit (the ground-unit attack range at
speed class 5, the war roll 1/700, the 20-unit main-fleet radius ±1, the planet target's centre term +1, the
auto-design penalty for tech 0x1d, the queue re-time clamp (no negative result), the `+0xad` byte in the queue shift
(always equal), `HajoDelete`'s fighter exemption (no carrier lost a ship), and a mirrored ship count only wrong above
three ships in a fleet record the AI then never reads. These are kept as specified from the asm.

## Precision notes (from the asm, all confirmed by the traces)
f32 throughout the fleet pass except: the speed class `(f64)(f32 expr) + 0.5`, the 1.3 / 1.5 / 2.0 strength tests
(f64), the scouting score `(f64)sqrtf(·)/3.0 + (f64)sqrtf(·)` rounded once, `MenekulesSuly`'s far branch (f64), the
flee directions' angle step (f64 add, rounded to f32 each step), `BezartSzog`'s `· 180.0f` then `/ π` in f64, and the
colonisation distance factor `((f64)r · 0.1 + 1.0) · (f64)d` (the spec first had it f32; the asm @ 0x50dadc says f64).
The libm calls (`sqrt`, `sin`, `cos`, `acos` on f64) match the exe's `libm_sse2_*_precise` on every traced value.
