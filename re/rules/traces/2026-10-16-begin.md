## Result

logic flags, counters, planet ids, `StoryError`, the undo list, `StoryAdat`, `CounterIntelligence_kem`), stream 5,
and every call the stories made, in order and with their arguments (fleets made, moved and deleted, ships and
units, money, messages and their parameters, buildings placed, planets taken, `TankDelete`, spy reports and the
nested check they run, the epidemic's `Virus_CheckAdd`); fleets' `+0x52` bits, planets' epidemic and `+0xbb`, the
human's view, the relations, trade agreements and offers, designs' prices, the techs made cheaper, building damage
and spies' targets are checked against `post`. 8 records read buildings the tracer didn't dump (story 10's search of
every planet before the dump covered it) and are counted, not checked. The older campaign traces still replay
exactly.

the event flags for the rest of the outer check: scripts get the whole chain (`Script::run_in`).
