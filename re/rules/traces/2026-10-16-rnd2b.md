## What the replay found
- The escorts' join loop runs over fleet slots 1..=`OsszHanyHajo` (the **ship** count). Slots past the fleet count
  hold heap garbage; `cvttss2si` turns a NaN or huge distance into INT_MIN, so those slots "join" the battle. The
  port truncates the same way; the tracer dumps the slots as `fext`.
- `st_AddFleetToSpWar` writes `spFlottak[n + 1]` from its own count: with a count below 2 it overwrites the battle's
  entries 1–2, which later stories in the same check read.
- 131's `st_bTav` rounds through f32 (the ELF doesn't).
- 151's building damage draws `rnd(5, 95)` a second time when the first falls short.
- A story conquest (`BolygoElfoglal` → exe 0x542980) cures the planet; with nothing infected left (no planet
  `+0xb8 > 0`, no fleet with `+0x52` bit 2 after the new owner's fleets within `d² < 100` are cleared) it sets
  `StoryAdat+0x30 := 0` and removes info 0x65 everywhere (`StoryWorld::conquest_ends_epidemic`).
- `HajoDelete` drops the units the fleet can no longer carry (nested `TankDelete`); `FlottaDelete` deletes the
  fleet's units.
